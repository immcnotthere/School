# Forward Premium and Discount

Forward rate above spot ($F > S$): the **denominator** currency is at a **forward premium**. Below spot ($F < S$): a **forward discount**.

Annualized: $\frac{F - S}{S} \times \frac{360}{N_{\text{days}}}$

Example: ¥100/\$ spot, ¥90/\$ 90-day forward. The dollar is at a 40% per annum forward discount.
