# Domestic vs International Bonds (Foreign Bond and Eurobond)

Domestic: issued and traded in the issuer's market. Foreign bond: issued in a domestic market by a foreign borrower, in the domestic currency. Eurobond: denominated in one or more currencies and traded outside the countries issuing them.
