# Uncovered Interest Rate Parity (UIP)

Same comparison as CIP but with no forward: the foreign money market investment has the same **expected** return as the domestic one.

$$1 + i = E_t\left[\frac{S(t+1)}{S(t)}\right] \times (1 + i^*)$$

**Unbiasedness hypothesis:** there is no systematic difference between the forward rate and the expected future spot rate, $F_t = E_t[S(t+1)]$. (CIP plus UIP together imply it.)

CIP vs UIP: CIP uses $F$ and is riskless; UIP uses the expected future spot and holds only on average.
