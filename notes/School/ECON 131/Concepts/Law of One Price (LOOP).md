# Law of One Price (LOOP)

In a currency, the same good sells for the same price everywhere. Violations: tariffs and quotas, transaction costs, noncompetitive markets, sticky prices.

$P(\text{home}) = S \times P(\text{foreign})$ for one good, $S$ in home per foreign.
