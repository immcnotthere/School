# Double-Entry Accounting (BOP)

Every transaction is a credit (inflow) and a debit (outflow) of equal value. Handout examples: Dell sells computers to Samsung; a Japanese buyer purchases a Santa Cruz condo; Budweiser swapped for Heineken.
