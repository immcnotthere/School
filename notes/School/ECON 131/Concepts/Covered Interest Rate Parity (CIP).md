# Covered Interest Rate Parity (CIP)

Investing at home must pay the same as converting at spot, investing abroad, and selling the proceeds forward:

$$1 + i = \frac{1}{S} \times (1 + i^*) \times F \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \frac{F}{S} = \frac{1 + i}{1 + i^*}$$

$S$ and $F$ are quoted as domestic currency per foreign currency; $i$ and $i^*$ are for the same period as the forward (divide annual rates by 4 for 3 months).

The higher-interest currency trades at a forward discount that offsets its interest advantage. It is riskless because the forward removes the currency risk. When it fails, see [[Covered Interest Arbitrage]].
