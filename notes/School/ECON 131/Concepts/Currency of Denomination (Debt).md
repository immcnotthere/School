# Currency of Denomination (Debt)

Borrow in the currency with the lower interest rate only if the currency does not appreciate enough to cancel it. Break-even: the future rate that makes expected interest cost the same in both currencies, $(1+i_{\$})=(1+i_{€})\,S_1/S_0$.
