# Carry Trade

A strategy in which an investor borrows in low-yield currencies and invests in high-yield currencies, **without** forward cover.

It earns the interest gap unless the high-yield currency depreciates by more than the gap, so it carries exchange rate risk. It is a bet that UIP fails.
