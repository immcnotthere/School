# Forward Hedge vs Money Market Hedge

Two ways to remove [[Transaction Exchange Risk]]:
- **Forward hedge:** lock in the rate today with a forward contract. No uncertainty.
- **Money market hedge:** for a foreign payable, borrow home currency now, buy the foreign currency at spot, and deposit it so it grows to exactly the amount owed. No currency mismatch at the payment date.

When CIP holds, the two cost the same. Zachy's Wine (€4M in 90 days): about \$4.32M either way.
