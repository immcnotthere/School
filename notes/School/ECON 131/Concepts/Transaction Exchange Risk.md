# Transaction Exchange Risk

The possibility of taking a loss in a foreign exchange transaction: you owe (or are owed) foreign currency on a future date, and the exchange rate moves against you before then.

Protection is **hedging**: see [[Forward Hedge vs Money Market Hedge]].
