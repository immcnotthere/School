# Covered Interest Arbitrage

When CIP fails, take the side with the higher covered return:
1. Borrow in the currency with the **lower** covered return.
2. Convert at spot.
3. Invest at the other currency's interest rate.
4. Sell the proceeds forward today.
5. At maturity, repay the loan; what is left is riskless profit.

Everyone doing this pushes $S$, $F$, $i$ and $i^*$ back toward parity, which is why CIP holds closely in practice.
