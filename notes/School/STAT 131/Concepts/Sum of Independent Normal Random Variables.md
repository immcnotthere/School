# Sum of Independent Normal Random Variables

If $X\sim Normal(\mu_x,\sigma_x^2)$ and $Y\sim Normal(\mu_y,\sigma_y^2)$ are independent, $W=X+Y\sim Normal(\mu_x+\mu_y,\sigma_x^2+\sigma_y^2)$: means and variances add.
