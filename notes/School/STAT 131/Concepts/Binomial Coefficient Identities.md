# Binomial Coefficient Identities

Key binomial coefficient facts: $\binom{n}{0}=\binom{n}{n}=1$, symmetry $\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n}{n-k}$, Pascal's rule $\binom{n}{k}+\binom{n}{k-1}=\binom{n+1}{k}$, and Vandermonde's identity $\binom{m+n}{k}=\sum_j\binom{m}{j}\binom{n}{k-j}$.
