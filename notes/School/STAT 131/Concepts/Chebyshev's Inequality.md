# Chebyshev's Inequality

For any $X$ with finite mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$, $P(|X-\mu|\ge\varepsilon)\le\sigma^2/\varepsilon^2$ for any $\varepsilon>0$, regardless of the distribution's shape.
