# Law of Total Probability

If $\{B_j\}$ partitions $\Omega$, then $P(A)=\sum_j P(B_j)P(A\mid B_j)$, with a conditional version $P(A\mid C)=\sum_j P(B_j\mid C)P(A\mid B_j\cap C)$.
