**10.7.23**

Suppose the $n \times n$ matrix function $A$ and the $n$-vector function $\mathbf{f}$ are continuous on $(a,b)$. Let $t_0 \in (a,b)$, let $\mathbf{k}$ be an arbitrary constant vector, and let $Y$ be a fundamental matrix for the homogeneous system $\mathbf{y}' = A(t)\mathbf{y}$. Use variation of parameters to show that the solution of the initial value problem
$$\mathbf{y}' = A(t)\mathbf{y} + \mathbf{f}(t),\quad \mathbf{y}(t_0) = \mathbf{k}$$
is
$$\mathbf{y}(t) = Y(t)\!\left(Y^{-1}(t_0)\mathbf{k} + \int_{t_0}^t Y^{-1}(s)\mathbf{f}(s)\,ds\right).$$
