# Position, Velocity and Acceleration

For position $x(t)$: velocity $v = \frac{dx}{dt}$ and acceleration $a = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{d^2x}{dt^2}$, so Newton's law reads $F = m\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}$.
