# Three Cases for Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

Distinct real eigenvalues each give one independent eigenvector; complex eigenvalues come in conjugate pairs; a repeated eigenvalue may give fewer independent eigenvectors than its multiplicity, requiring a generalized eigenvector.
