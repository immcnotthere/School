# Deriving the Falling Object Equation

Combine gravity $mg$ and drag $-\gamma v$ with Newton's second law to derive $m\frac{dv}{dt}=mg-\gamma v$, then reduce to $\frac{dv}{dt}=-\frac{1}{5}(v-49)$ for $m=10$, $\gamma=2$.
