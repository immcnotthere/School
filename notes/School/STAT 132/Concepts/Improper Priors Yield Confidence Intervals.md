# Improper Priors Yield Confidence Intervals

Under the usual improper prior, a Bayesian posterior interval for $\mu$ (or $\sigma^2$) is numerically identical to the corresponding classical confidence interval, because the pivotal's sampling and posterior distributions coincide.
