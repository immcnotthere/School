# t Distribution of the Sample Mean

For an iid normal sample, $n^{1/2}(\bar X_n-\mu)/\check\sigma$ has the t distribution with n-1 df, where $\check\sigma$ uses an n-1 denominator; this removes dependence on the unknown sigma.
