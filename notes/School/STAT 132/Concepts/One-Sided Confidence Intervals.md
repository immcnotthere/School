# One-Sided Confidence Intervals

A one-sided coefficient $\gamma$ CI has the form $(A,\infty)$ or $(-\infty,B)$; for a normal mean, $A = X_n - T_{n-1}^{-1}(\gamma)\hat\sigma/\sqrt n$.
