# Power of the t Test

Power is $1-T_{n-1}(c\mid\psi)$ (one-sided) or a two-tail analog, a function of $\mu,\sigma$ only through $\psi=\sqrt n(\mu-\mu_0)/\sigma$; used to choose sample size.
