# Law of large numbers

As $n\to\infty$, $\bar X_n \to E[X]$.
This is about **where** the average goes. Individual observations stay just as variable; only the *average* stabilizes.
Contrast with [[Central Limit Theorem]], which is about the *shape* of $\bar X$'s distribution.
