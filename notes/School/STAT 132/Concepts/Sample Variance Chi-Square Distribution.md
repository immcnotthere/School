# Sample Variance Chi-Square Distribution

For a normal sample, $\sum(X_i-\bar X_n)^2/\sigma^2$ (equivalently $n\hat\sigma^2/\sigma^2$) has the chi-square distribution with $n-1$ degrees of freedom.
