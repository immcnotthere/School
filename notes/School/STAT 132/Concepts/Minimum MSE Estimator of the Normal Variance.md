# Minimum MSE Estimator of the Normal Variance

Among estimators $T_c=c\sum(X_i-X_n)^2$ for a normal sample, $c=1/(n+1)$ minimizes the M.S.E. for every $\mu,\sigma^2$, beating both $\hat\sigma_0^2$ and $\hat\sigma_1^2$.
