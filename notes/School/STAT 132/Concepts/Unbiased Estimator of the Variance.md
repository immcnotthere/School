# Unbiased Estimator of the Variance

$\hat\sigma_1^2=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum(X_i-X_n)^2$ is unbiased for $Var_\theta(X_1)$ for a general distribution; the divide-by-$n$ version $\hat\sigma_0^2$ is biased low by a factor $(n-1)/n$.
