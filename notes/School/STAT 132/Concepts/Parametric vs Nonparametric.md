# Parametric vs. nonparametric

- **Parametric:** the population is in a family indexed by finitely many parameters (e.g. $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$). It is efficient **if** the assumption is right.
- **Nonparametric:** no fixed finite-parameter family (e.g. estimating the median with the sample median).
"Nonparametric" ≠ no parameters and ≠ no assumptions. The trade-off is precision vs. robustness.
