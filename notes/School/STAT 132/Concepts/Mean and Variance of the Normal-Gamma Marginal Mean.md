# Mean and Variance of the Normal-Gamma Marginal Mean

If $\alpha_0>1/2$, $E(\mu)=\mu_0$; if $\alpha_0>1$, $Var(\mu)=\beta_0/[\lambda_0(\alpha_0-1)]$.
