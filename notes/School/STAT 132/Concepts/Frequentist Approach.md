# Frequentist approach

The parameter is **fixed**. Probability describes **data and procedures under repeated sampling**.
The 95% CI means: *if we repeat the sampling and the method many times, about 95% of the intervals cover $\theta$.*
Any single computed interval either contains $\theta$ or it doesn't. You can't say "95% chance $\theta$ is in [a,b]."
Methods are judged by long-run performance: coverage, Type I error, variance.
Compare [[Bayesian Approach]].
