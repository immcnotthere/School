# Marginal Distribution of the Mean (Normal-Gamma)

Under a normal-gamma distribution, $(\lambda_0\alpha_0/\beta_0)^{1/2}(\mu-\mu_0)$ has the t distribution with $2\alpha_0$ degrees of freedom.
