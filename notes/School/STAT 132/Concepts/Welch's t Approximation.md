# Welch's t Approximation

Approximates the distribution of $V=(\bar X_m-\bar Y_n)/[S_X^2/(m(m-1))+S_Y^2/(n(n-1))]^{1/2}$ by a t distribution with Welch-Satterthwaite degrees of freedom.
