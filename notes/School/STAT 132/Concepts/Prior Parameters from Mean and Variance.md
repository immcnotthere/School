# Prior Parameters from Mean and Variance

Match the family's mean and variance formulas to the given numbers and solve. Gamma: $\beta = m/v$, $\alpha = m^2/v$. Beta: $s = \alpha+\beta$ from $v = m(1-m)/(s+1)$, then $\alpha = ms$, $\beta = (1-m)s$.
