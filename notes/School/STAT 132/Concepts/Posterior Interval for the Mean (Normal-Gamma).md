# Posterior Interval for the Mean (Normal-Gamma)

A $1-\alpha$ posterior interval for $\mu$ is $\mu_1\pm T_{2\alpha_1}^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)\sqrt{\beta_1/(\alpha_1\lambda_1)}$.
