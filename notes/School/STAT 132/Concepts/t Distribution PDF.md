# t Distribution PDF

The pdf of the t distribution with m df is $g(x)=\Gamma(\frac{m+1}{2})/[(m\pi)^{1/2}\Gamma(\frac{m}{2})]\,(1+x^2/m)^{-(m+1)/2}$.
