# The goals of inference

1. **Point estimation:** one best guess, e.g. $\hat p = 84/120 = .70$. It hides uncertainty.
2. **Interval estimation:** a range plus an uncertainty measure. A confidence interval ([[Frequentist Approach]]) and a credible interval ([[Bayesian Approach]]) mean different things.
3. **Prediction:** a *future observation*, not a parameter. It must add natural variation on top of parameter uncertainty.
4. **Hypothesis testing:** e.g. $H_0:\mu=100$ vs $H_1:\mu\ne100$. A test doesn't *prove* anything; it controls error rates.
