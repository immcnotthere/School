# Confidence Interval for the Mean of a Normal Distribution

For a normal sample with unknown $\sigma$, $X_n \pm T_{n-1}^{-1}((1+\gamma)/2)\hat\sigma/\sqrt n$ is an exact coefficient $\gamma$ CI for $\mu$.
