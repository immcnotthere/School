# Power of Two-Sample t Test

Under $\mu_1\neq\mu_2$, U has the noncentral t distribution with $m+n-2$ df and noncentrality $\psi=(\mu_1-\mu_2)/[\sigma(1/m+1/n)^{1/2}]$.
