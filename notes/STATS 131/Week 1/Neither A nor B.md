## Summary
"Neither $A$ nor $B$" means both complements happen.

## Event form
$$
\text{Neither }A\text{ nor }B \equiv A^c\cap B^c
$$

## Formula
$$
P(A^c\cap B^c)=1-P(A\cup B)
$$

## Example
If $P(A\cup B)=0.72$, then:
$$
P(A^c\cap B^c)=1-0.72=0.28
$$

## Common mistakes
- Writing "neither" as $A^c\cup B^c$ (wrong).
