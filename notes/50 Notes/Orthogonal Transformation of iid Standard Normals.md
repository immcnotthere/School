# Orthogonal Transformation of iid Standard Normals

If $X_1,\dots,X_n$ are iid standard normal and $\mathbf Y=A\mathbf X$ for orthogonal $A$, then $Y_1,\dots,Y_n$ are also iid standard normal; choosing the first row as $1/\sqrt n$ isolates $\bar X_n$.
