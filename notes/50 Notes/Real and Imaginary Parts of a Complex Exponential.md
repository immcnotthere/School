# Real and Imaginary Parts of a Complex Exponential

Splitting $e^{(\alpha+i\beta)t}=e^{\alpha t}\cos\beta t + i\,e^{\alpha t}\sin\beta t$ gives the two real solutions produced by a complex characteristic root $\alpha\pm i\beta$.
