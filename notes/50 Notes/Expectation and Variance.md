# Expectation and variance

$E[X]=\sum x\,P(X=x)$ or $\int x f(x)\,dx$. This is the long-run average, and it need not be a possible value.

$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E[(X-E X)^2]=E[X^2]-(E X)^2$. $\mathrm{SD}=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}}$ has the same units as $X$.

Two distributions can have the same mean and very different spread.
