## Quiz information

### Point estimator
- A point estimator is a statistic: a formula that turns the sample into ONE number used as a guess for a parameter.
- Examples: sample mean (X bar) for the population mean, sample proportion (p hat) for the population proportion, sample variance (S squared) for the population variance.
- Not a point estimator: a confidence interval. It gives a range, so it is an interval estimator.
- Estimator vs estimate: the estimator is the formula (random, before data); the estimate is the number it gives for your data.
