# Conditional PDF

For continuous $X,Y$, $f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=f_{X,Y}(x,y)/f_Y(y)$ when $f_Y(y)>0$; probabilities of $X$ given $Y=y$ are obtained by integrating this conditional density.
