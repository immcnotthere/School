# Leibniz Notation for Higher Derivatives

$\frac{d^nx}{dt^n}$ means differentiate $x$ with respect to $t$, $n$ times; the exponent-looking $n$ counts derivatives and squares nothing. Same as $x^{(n)}$; in physics $\dot x$, $\ddot x$.
