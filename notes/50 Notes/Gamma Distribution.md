# Gamma Distribution

The Gamma$(\alpha,\beta)$ distribution generalizes the exponential ($\alpha=1$) with $E[X]=\alpha/\beta$, $\text{Var}(X)=\alpha/\beta^2$.

Cards: [[STAT 132 Cards - Reading01#Gamma Distribution]]

The function Γ only appears inside the distribution's constant β^α/Γ(α).
