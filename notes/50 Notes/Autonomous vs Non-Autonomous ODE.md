# Autonomous vs Non-Autonomous ODE

An ODE $dy/dt=f(y)$ is autonomous (no explicit $t$); $dy/dt=f(t,y)$ is non-autonomous; any non-autonomous scalar equation can be made autonomous by promoting $t$ to a second unknown function.
