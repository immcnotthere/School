# Bayesian approach

**Uncertainty** about $\theta$ is described by a probability distribution: [[Prior Distribution]] → data → [[Posterior Distribution]].
This allows direct statements like "95% posterior probability that $\theta\in[a,b]$" (a credible interval).
$\theta$ isn't physically random. The probability represents our *information*.

**Shared with frequentist:** probability models for data, the [[Likelihood Function]], and dependence on good sampling and models.
