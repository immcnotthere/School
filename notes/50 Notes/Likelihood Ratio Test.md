# Likelihood Ratio Test

Rejects $H_0$ when the likelihood ratio $\Lambda(x)=\sup_{\Theta_0}f_n/\sup_{\Theta}f_n$ is small; $-2\log\Lambda$ is asymptotically chi-square.
