# Amplitude-Phase Form of a Sinusoid

A sum $c_1\cos\theta+c_2\sin\theta$ equals a single shifted cosine $R\cos(\theta-\delta)$, where $R$ is the norm of $[c_1,c_2]^T$ and $\tan\delta=c_2/c_1$.
