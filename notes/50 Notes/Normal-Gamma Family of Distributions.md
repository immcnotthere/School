# Normal-Gamma Family of Distributions

A joint distribution for $(\mu,\tau)$ where $\mu\mid\tau\sim N(\mu_0,\lambda_0\tau)$ and $\tau\sim\text{Gamma}(\alpha_0,\beta_0)$; the conjugate family for normal data with unknown mean and precision.
