# Bayes' Theorem for Distributions

$\pi(\theta\mid\mathbf{x})=\dfrac{f(\mathbf{x}\mid\theta)\pi(\theta)}{m_n(\mathbf{x})}$; the marginal $m_n(\mathbf{x})$ normalizes the likelihood-times-prior into a valid posterior distribution.
