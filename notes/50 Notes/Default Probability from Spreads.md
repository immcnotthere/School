# Default Probability from Spreads

If the bond pays in full with probability $1-p$ and nothing uncollateralized with probability $p$, then price $=$ (collateralized PV) $+\,(1-p)\times$ (PV of the rest at the risk-free spot rates). Solve for $p$.
