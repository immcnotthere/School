# Sample Mean

The sample mean $\bar X_n=\frac1n\sum X_i$ of an iid sample with mean $\mu$, variance $\sigma^2$ has $E[\bar X_n]=\mu$ and $V(\bar X_n)=\sigma^2/n$.
