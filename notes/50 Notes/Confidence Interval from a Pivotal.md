# Confidence Interval from a Pivotal

Given a pivotal $V$ with continuous cdf $G$ and invertible $r(v,x)$ with $r(V,X)=g(\theta)$, the endpoints $A=r(G^{-1}(\gamma_1),X)$, $B=r(G^{-1}(\gamma_2),X)$ form a coefficient $\gamma=\gamma_2-\gamma_1$ CI.
