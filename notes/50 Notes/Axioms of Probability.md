# Axioms of Probability

A probability measure $\mathbb{P}$ satisfies $0 \le \mathbb{P}(A) \le 1$, $\mathbb{P}(\Omega)=1$, and additivity over disjoint events.

## Merged from Probability Axioms

A probability measure satisfies $0 \le P(A) \le 1$, $P(\Omega)=1$, and additivity over disjoint events; probabilities are interpreted as long-run proportions.
