# Bond Pricing (Coupon Bond)

$PV=\sum_{t=1}^{n}\dfrac{\text{coupon}}{(1+r)^t}+\dfrac{\text{Face}}{(1+r)^n}=\text{coupon}\times\dfrac{(1+r)^n-1}{r(1+r)^n}+\dfrac{\text{Face}}{(1+r)^n}$.
