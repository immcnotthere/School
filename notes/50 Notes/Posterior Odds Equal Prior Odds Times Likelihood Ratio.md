# Posterior Odds Equal Prior Odds Times Likelihood Ratio

For two candidates, the data's whole contribution is the likelihood ratio.

Example: prior odds 0.3/0.7 = 0.43; likelihood ratio 0.082/0.031 = 2.6; posterior odds 1.12, so 1.12/2.12 = 53% for the 80% shooter.
