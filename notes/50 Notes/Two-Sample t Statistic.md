# Two-Sample t Statistic

$U=(m+n-2)^{1/2}(\bar X_m-\bar Y_n)/[(1/m+1/n)^{1/2}(S_X^2+S_Y^2)^{1/2}]$ has the $t_{m+n-2}$ distribution when $\mu_1=\mu_2$ and variances are equal.
