# Separation of Variables

To solve $dy/dt=k(y-a)$, divide by $(y-a)$ assuming $y\neq a$, integrate both sides to get $\ln|y-a|=kt+c$, exponentiate, and absorb constants to reach the general solution $y=a+Ce^{kt}$.
