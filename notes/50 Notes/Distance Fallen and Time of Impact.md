# Distance Fallen and Time of Impact

Integrate $v(t)$ to get position $x(t)=49t-245(1-e^{-t/5})$, then solve $x(t_g)=300$ numerically for the impact time $t_g\approx10.51$ s and impact speed $\approx43.01$ m/sec.
