# Precision of a Normal Distribution

The precision $\tau$ of a normal distribution is $1/\sigma^2$; it reparameterizes the normal pdf as $f(x\mid\mu,\tau)=(\tau/2\pi)^{1/2}\exp(-\tau(x-\mu)^2/2)$.
